Contextual callout used for withdrawal-window / eligibility feedback.

```jsx
<Alert variant="success" title="Within your window" description="You're eligible to withdraw now." />
<Alert variant="warning" title="Monthly limit nearly used" description="Remaining ₹700 · Minimum ₹1,000." />
<Alert variant="error" title="Fee is higher than request" description="Increase your withdrawal amount." />
<Alert variant="info" title="Outside application window" description="You can apply between Day 1 and Day 25." />
```

4 variants: `info` (blue), `success` (green), `warning` (orange), `error` (red) — each pairs a tinted card background with a solid icon badge (i / ✓ / ! / ×).
